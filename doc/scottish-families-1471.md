# Scottish households at the 1471 campaign start

Scope: the 36 `dadg_sco_lord_clan_*` owners of the Scottish clans in `DellarteDellaGuerraMap`, their spouses and children already born by the early 1471 start. This audit does not cover English families. The 1471 holder is kept in the existing clan owner ID so settlements and clan references still resolve.

`c.` means a year estimated by a cited historical account. `~` means an **uncorroborated game age estimate**, calculated as 1471 minus the NPC age. `?` means no defensible date was found. An age estimate is required by the NPC format and must not be read as a historical birth record. The Hero XML supports `alive`, parents, and spouse, but has no birth-date or future death-date field. Historical death dates below are documentation, not scripted deaths. Children born after the start are intentionally absent.

James IV, the first child of James III and Margaret of Denmark, was born in **1473**. Mary Stewart did not marry the widowed James Hamilton until **1473/74**; their sons James and Robert were born later. These people must not be children or spouses in a 1471 start. Gilbert Kennedy also married Isabel Ogilvy after Catherine Maxwell, but the date of that second marriage and Catherine's death do not establish which woman was his wife in early 1471. Neither is assigned as his current spouse. The clan genealogies also list later spouses and children whose existence by 1471 cannot be established, so they are not fabricated here. A dead former spouse (Euphemia Graham) is included only to preserve Elizabeth Hamilton's known parentage.

## Feudal title configuration

The `config/titles.config.xml` hierarchy is partly a gameplay projection onto the campaign's fixed towns and castles, not a literal roll of the Scottish peerage. Its historically named 1471 dignities now distinguish title from settlement ownership: the Crown holds Moray from the 1455 forfeiture and Orkney from William Sinclair's 1470 resignation; John MacDonald holds Ross from his 1449 succession; and William Keith's comital title is displayed as Earldom Marischal rather than a fictitious County of Aberdeen. The stable title IDs and seats are retained for save/config compatibility.

## Douglas of Lochleven addition, 3 November 2026

Lochleven now belongs to a separate landed household, not the unrelated Angus
branch. Robert Douglas and Elizabeth Boswell use game ages 45 and 43 because
reliable birth years were not found. The 1463 grant reserved Henry Douglas's
liferent and Elizabeth Erskine's terce; Henry was still alive in January 1468/69,
and Robert's heir precept is dated 1476. The exact succession date is therefore
not asserted. Elizabeth Boswell's survival into 1471 is provisional. Children with
uncertain birth dates are omitted. Robert is not styled Sir: the source only
establishes his knighthood by 1504. The banner reuses the existing plain Douglas
family arms as a gameplay representation, without claiming a securely dated
1471 Lochleven-specific difference.

St Andrews remains unchanged pending the church branch; see the
[episcopal ownership handoff](church-st-andrews-1471.md).
