# Starting ownership audit, spring 1471

Reviewed and applied 3 November 2026. This corrects 26 of the 27 differences found by
`StartingOwnershipOverRealContentTests` between the live Map settlement owners and
the Core title configuration. The date is the Readeption, before Barnet and
Tewkesbury. Later Yorkist grants must not be projected backward into this start.
St Andrews is deliberately deferred to the church branch, at the user's request.
Its existing Lindsay settlement owner and Stewart regional title placeholder remain
unchanged; neither represents its historical episcopal lordship.

## Policy and limits

The existing roster combines houses, not every individual estate owner. A castle
keeper is not automatically its hereditary owner. A geographic County/Duchy tier
is not automatically the dignity of its holder, and a peerage name does not establish
private ownership of its eponymous town. The table below distinguishes supported
estate assignments from the regional and family proxies retained by this roster.

26 settlement owners changed; St Andrews was left unchanged. Wardour's title holder also changed from Lovell
to the restored Crown, with a Strong Lovell claim. Skipton's Stanley acquisition
date changed from 1461 to 1465. Lochleven now has its own Douglas of Lochleven
household, led by Robert Douglas, with its grant dated 1463. No seats, coordinates, territorial parents, title IDs
or dignity ranks changed. Exeter and Launceston's descriptions no longer name their
previous, unsupported owners.

These are starting-data corrections. Existing campaigns retain their ownership and
title state; start a new campaign to use this arrangement.

## Regression checks

`StartingOwnershipOverRealContentTests` reads the live title and settlement XML on
each invocation, including with `--no-build`, checks the referenced clans and all
139 title seats, and reports every unexpected difference. Its only exception
pins the exact St Andrews pair (Stewart title / Lindsay settlement); it must fail
if that pair changes without reviewing/removing the exception. It has no blanket
exceptions for claims. `PoliticalMapTerritoriesOverRealContentTests` independently
pins the reviewed assignments, so changing both XML owners to the same wrong clan
does not silently pass. These checks protect consistency with this reviewed start;
they do not prove every proxy is a literal estate ownership or verify on-screen labels.

Wardour's change also moves Lovell's primary estate to Northampton, which follows
Warwick under the existing territorial fallback. Succession, blood-claim and private-war
content expectations must reflect the changed portfolio and its retained claim.

Run from Core/src after building:

```powershell
rtk proxy dotnet test DellarteDellaGuerra.Infrastructure.Tests/DellarteDellaGuerra.Infrastructure.Tests.csproj -c Release --no-build --filter "FullyQualifiedName~Titles|FullyQualifiedName~PoliticalMap"
```

The focused Infrastructure.Tests `Titles|PoliticalMap` run passed **140/140**,
